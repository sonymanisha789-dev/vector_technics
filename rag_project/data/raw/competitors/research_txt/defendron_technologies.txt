# DEFENDRON TECHNOLOGIES — RESEARCH REPORT
Compiled 24 September 2026. All findings sourced from public web material; gaps are flagged explicitly rather than filled in.

## 0. IDENTIFICATION AND SCOPE NOTE

The name "Defendron Technologies" resolves to exactly one real entity: **Defendron Technologies Private Limited**, an Indian defence-electronics startup in Pune, Maharashtra, trading as **DEFENDRON** at https://www.defendrontech.com.

Two disambiguation points matter before anything else:

1. This is **not** a counter-drone (C-UAS) company, despite the name suggesting "defend" + "drone". It builds **EO/IR (electro-optical / infrared) ISR payloads that mount onto drones** — the sensor turret, not the interceptor. Searches for "Defendron drone defense" surface unrelated counter-UAS firms (Dedrone/Axon, DroneShield, WhiteFox Defense, Drone Defence UK) which have no connection to it.
2. It is frequently confused in search with the similarly-named Australian firm **DefendTex** (https://www.defendtex.com/air.html), which makes the Drone 40 loitering munition. Unrelated company.

Coverage caveat: this is a company roughly 23 months old with essentially no press coverage, no analyst coverage, no filings beyond statutory Indian registry minimums, and no announced funding rounds.

Company's own Investor page (defendrontech.com/investor) names three backers, no amounts or dates disclosed:
1. **All In Capital** — India pre-seed/seed VC (founded by Kushal Bhagia). Implies a pre-seed/seed cheque in the ₹1–4 Cr range typical for this fund, but unconfirmed.
2. **IIMA Ventures** — incubator/early-stage platform at IIM Ahmedabad (formerly CIIE.CO). Could be incubation, accelerator, grant, or small equity.
3. **MeitY Startup Hub (MSH)** — Government of India programme, typically non-dilutive grant/incubation support.

Third-party contradiction: Tracxn tags the company "Unfunded" — no round in Tracxn's database, and zero funding-round press coverage found (Inc42, Entrackr, YourStory, VCCircle, Business Standard all silent). Most likely explanation: small, unannounced early-stage support never press-released.

Sector context: Indian defence-tech funding reached USD 247M in 2025 (~2x 2024's USD 125M) across 30 rounds, dominated by large rounds like Raphe mPhibr's Series B (General Catalyst). Defendron is far below this radar — a pre-seed micro-cap.

## 5. PRODUCTS

Single product category: EO/IR gimballed ISR payloads for UAVs, five current models (160g–1500g). Also a secondary custom-test-rig line for defence-systems validation (not listed as a catalogue product, mentioned only in press).

Note: product line was renumbered — old DTX-250/DTX-500 URLs now 404, mapped to new DTX-350/DTX-750 HDIR naming. Preserved here as a known source-conflict.

| Model | Weight | Mech. axes | EO optical zoom | IR resolution | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DTX Dual SO | 160 g | 0 (electronic stabilisation only) | 2x | 640×480 | Available |
| DTX KZ | 220 g | 1 | 2x | 640×480 (or 320×240 cost-down) | Out of stock |
| DTX-350 | 420 g | 2 | 20x | 640×480 | Available |
| DTX-750 HDIR | 790 g | 2 (assumed) | 30x | 1280×720 HD | Available |
| DTX-1500 | 1500 g | 2 | 40x | 1280×720 HD + 4x IR zoom | Available |

All models: onboard edge AI target detection/lock/classification; 3-axis stabilisation (2 mechanical + 1 electronic, or all-electronic on the smallest); MAVLink compatible; H.264/H.265 RTP/RTSP over Ethernet; 12-24V power; -20°C to +55°C operating range; IP55 rated (homepage claims IP67 — inconsistent with per-product IP55 specs); onboard recording; digital zoom to 8x; customisable configurations on request.

Stated use cases: border/perimeter surveillance, military reconnaissance, maritime patrol, counter-UAS ops, critical infrastructure protection, disaster response, loitering-munition/interceptor guidance (DTX Dual SO and DTX KZ descriptions explicitly reference "urban neutralisation," "interceptor swarm," and "terminal navigation" — i.e., munition seeker use).

No pricing published for any product.

## 8. SUMMARY ASSESSMENT

Defendron Technologies is a pre-revenue-or-near-pre-revenue, pre-seed-stage Indian defence hardware startup, roughly 23 months old, with a six-person team operating from a Pune coworking space, a five-model EO/IR payload catalogue that has already been renumbered once, three self-reported early-stage/government backers with undisclosed terms, no announced funding, no named customers, no verifiable patents, and exactly one piece of meaningful press coverage (Inc42, June 2025). Real and technically credible on paper, but at the very earliest stage of its public life.
