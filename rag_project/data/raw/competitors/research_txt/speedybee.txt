## 0. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY / KEY CORRECTIONS

Three premises in the research brief need correcting before anything else:

1. **SpeedyBee is not headquartered in Shenzhen.** Its operating legal entity is registered in **Zhuhai, Guangdong Province**, at 珠海市高新区港湾大道田馆路12号厂房四楼A区 (4th Floor, Area A, Building 4, No. 12 Tianguan Road, Gangwan Avenue, Gaoxin/High-Tech District, Zhuhai). Source: [水滴信用 registry record](https://m.shuidi.cn/company-91c66a46b7a78c6a34bf7fb14f7c1901.html), [爱查ICP registry record](https://www.aichaicp.com/cd-91c66a46b7a78c6a34bf7fb14f7c1901.html), [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/speedybee/__tadsCzWnNqxzY6kybm9CSBAr75hDILWtlDm-IkDTNdM), [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/speedybee). The Shenzhen association is real but indirect — see the RunCam relationship in §1.3.

2. **SpeedyBee has raised no venture funding, ever.** There is no round, no investor, no valuation, no filing. Tracxn states flatly: "Speedybee has not raised any funding yet," and records zero investments and zero acquisitions by the company. This is not a data gap — it is a private, founder-owned, cash-funded Chinese hardware business.

3. **The "founded 2005" date circulating in Western databases is wrong.** [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/speedybee/__tadsCzWnNqxzY6kybm9CSBAr75hDILWtlDm-IkDTNdM) lists 2005, which is impossible — the consumer FPV market did not exist in a recognizable form then, and SpeedyBee's first reviewed product dates to 2018. The Chinese business registry gives the authoritative incorporation date of the current entity: **6 January 2021**. The brand itself predates that entity by roughly three years (first products mid-2018), which is explained by the RunCam relationship.

### 1.2 Brand history and origin

SpeedyBee's real history is best reconstructed from the review record, because the company publishes almost nothing about itself. The most complete public chronology is Oscar Liang's archive ([oscarliang.com search](https://oscarliang.com/?s=speedybee)), which spans 10 pages of SpeedyBee coverage from 2018 to 2025.

**Phase 0 — 2017/2018, OEM camera era.** The earliest SpeedyBee-labelled item reviewed is the "[Eachine SpeedyBee SEC FPV Camera](https://oscarliang.com/page/3/?s=speedybee)" (2 January 2018), sold under the Eachine banner and positioned as a budget racing camera against RunCam and Foxeer. SpeedyBee at this point was a design house, not a brand with its own retail presence.

**Phase 1 — 2018, the Bluetooth insight.** The founding product idea was not a drone part at all; it was *removing the laptop from the field*. In June 2018 SpeedyBee shipped the **F4 AIO FC with Bluetooth**, described by Liang as "the first of its kind to have a built-in bluetooth module," allowing pilots to configure a quad from a phone. One month later (18 July 2018) came the **USB-to-Bluetooth Adapter**, solving the same problem for flight controllers that had no spare UART. Sources: [Oscar Liang, page 3](https://oscarliang.com/page/3/?s=speedybee).

This is the company's actual strategic DNA and it explains everything since: SpeedyBee is a *configuration-experience* company that sells hardware, which is why its corporate registration says "software and IT services" and why the **SpeedyBee App** — not any single board — is the real moat.

**Phase 2 — 2019, video transmission.** The **TX500 VTX** (24 January 2019), a 20x20mm-stack-fitting VTX with built-in mic and VTX control, and the **DVR VTX** (25 January 2019), a ~600mW transmitter with onboard DVR recording to survive signal breakup. Source: [Oscar Liang, page 3](https://oscarliang.com/page/3/?s=speedybee).

**Phase 3 — 2020, the Adapter becomes a category.** The **WiFi Adapter 2** (7 August 2020) moved from Bluetooth to WiFi and let pilots run full Betaflight setup from a phone alone. Liang later called the Adapter 2 "the MUST HAVE tool" ([Adapter 3 review](https://oscarliang.com/speedybee-adapter-3/)). This product made the brand.

**Phase 4 — 2021, incorporation and the flight-controller pivot.** The Zhuhai entity is incorporated 6 January 2021. In March 2021 the **F7 V2 FC stack** launched with integrated Bluetooth — "you don't need a computer" ([Oscar Liang, page 2](https://oscarliang.com/page/2/?s=speedybee)). The V2 used a dual-MCU design: STM32F722 for the FC plus a dedicated STM32F051 for ESC duties ([SoarSky lineup comparison](https://soarskyrc.com/blogs/technology-sharing/fpv-flight-controller-complete-guide-how-it-works-key-specs-amp-speedybee-full-lineup-comparison)).

**Phase 5 — 2022, the value-stack breakout.** October 2022 was the company's decisive month: **F405 V3** (26 Oct, "the best value stack"), **F7 V3** (27 Oct, US$119.99 full stack), **Adapter 3** (21 Oct), and **TX800 VTX** (20 Oct) all landed within eight days, plus the **Flex25 whoop frame** in July ("possibly the smallest 2.5″ whoop ever existed"). Source: [Oscar Liang, page 2](https://oscarliang.com/page/2/?s=speedybee). The F405 V3 became, by common consensus in subsequent coverage, the default budget 5-inch stack of its generation.

**Phase 6 — 2023, verticalization into complete aircraft.** SpeedyBee stopped selling only components and began selling flyable drones and frames: **Master 5 V2 BNF** (19 July 2023), **Goggles BEC** (31 May 2023), **F405 Mini stack** (15 Aug 2023), **F405 WING APP** (25 Dec 2023 — its first fixed-wing FC, "currently the cheapest wing FC you can get"), **Bee35 Cinewhoop** (27 Dec 2023), **Mario Fold 8** long-range folding drone (30 Dec 2023). Sources: [page 1](https://oscarliang.com/?s=speedybee), [page 2](https://oscarliang.com/page/2/?s=speedybee).

**Phase 7 — 2024, platform breadth.** **TX1600 Ultra VTX** (19 Jan), **F405 Wing Mini** (18 Feb), **Mario 5 frame** (28 Feb, pitched against the TBS Source One), **F405 V4** (13 Apr), **Bee25 Cinewhoop** (21 Oct, built around DJI O3), **Mario 5 complete drone** (11 Nov), **F405 AIO** (18 Nov).

**Phase 8 — 2025–2026, modularity and O4.** **F405 V5 OX32** (30 Aug 2025) and the current modular **Mario MiniX** / **Master 3X** / **Bee25** / **Bee35** families built around DJI O3 and O4 air units ([speedybee.com](https://www.speedybee.com/)).

### 1.3 The RunCam relationship (important and under-reported)

The US trademark **"SPEEDY BEE"** (Serial No. 88478719, Registration No. 6121981, filed 18 June 2019) is registered not to the Zhuhai entity but to **RunCam Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd.** — covering downloadable software for controlling and analyzing flight-controller parameter settings, vehicle radios, and cases. Source: [Justia Trademarks](https://trademarks.justia.com/884/78/speedy-88478719.html).

Corroborating evidence of the tie:
- SpeedyBee's own storefront hosts RunCam product pages, e.g. [RunCam 5 Orange](https://www.speedybee.com/runcam-5-orange/) and [RunCam Thumb](https://www.speedybee.com/runcam-thumb/), under "PARTNER PRODUCTS" sections for [action cameras](https://www.speedybee.com/action-camera/) and [analog FPV systems](https://www.speedybee.com/analog-camera/).
- SpeedyBee's own BNF drones ship with RunCam optics as standard: the Master 5 V2 uses a **RunCam Phoenix 2 SE** ([review](https://oscarliang.com/speedybee-master-5-v2-drone/)), and the Bee35 analog variant is sold at Pyrodrone explicitly as "(RunCam Phonix SE)" ([Pyrodrone](https://pyrodrone.com/collections/speedybee-products)).
- The SpeedyBee App's camera firmware flasher lists RunCam hardware as its example targets — "RunCam Hybrid, Racer2 etc." ([SpeedyBee App page](https://www.speedybee.com/speedy-bee-app/)).
- Product launches were historically synchronized — the SpeedyBee Adapter 2 launched alongside the RunCam Split 4 and RunCam Webcam.

**Interpretation, stated as inference not fact:** SpeedyBee originated inside or immediately adjacent to RunCam's orbit as a software/accessory brand around 2018–2019 (hence RunCam holding the US mark for the *software* class), and was subsequently spun into its own Zhuhai operating company in January 2021 as the hardware business scaled. The two now operate as sister/partner brands with deliberately non-overlapping catalogs: RunCam owns cameras, SpeedyBee owns flight controllers, ESCs, VTX, frames, adapters and the app. Separate storefronts (shop.runcam.com vs speedybee.com) obscure this from most buyers.

Caveat: the US mark's ownership has **not** been confirmed as reassigned to the Zhuhai entity. Meanwhile the Zhuhai entity is filing its own marks in China (see §6.2). A reader should not assume a current parent-subsidiary structure — only a documented shared-IP history.

### 2.1 Indirect financial indicators (the only quantitative proxies available)

Since no statements exist, here are the defensible proxies:

- **132 social-insurance enrollees** ([爱查ICP](https://www.aichaicp.com/cd-91c66a46b7a78c6a34bf7fb14f7c1901.html)). For a Guangdong hardware SME with a design-and-outsource model, this is a mid-size operation, not a garage shop.
- **81 concurrent open roles** (ibid.) — inconsistent with a contracting business; suggests active expansion as of mid-2026.
- **Catalog breadth as revenue proxy:** at least 43 distinct SKUs at a single US retailer ([Pyrodrone](https://pyrodrone.com/collections/speedybee-products)), 15 at [NewBeeDrone](https://newbeedrone.com/collections/speedybee), 11 flight controllers alone on the first-party site ([speedybee.com/flight-controller](https://www.speedybee.com/flight-controller/)), spanning US$0.99 (a beeper) to US$611.99 (a Bee35 PRO HD complete drone).
- **Price band:** components cluster US$30–140; complete drones US$249–612. The company plays the high-volume mid-market, not the premium niche.
- **Distribution footprint:** first-party e-commerce (speedybee.com), AliExpress, Amazon, plus Western specialist retail — GetFPV, RaceDayQuads (RDQ), Pyrodrone, NewBeeDrone, KiwiQuads (NZ), SoarSky. Multi-channel global distribution across at least three continents.
- **Paid-in capital equals registered capital** — both RMB 5M, contributed 19 January 2024 in cash (货币) per the 2025 annual report ([爱查ICP](https://www.aichaicp.com/cd-91c66a46b7a78c6a34bf7fb14f7c1901.html)). A later-dated full cash paydown is a mild positive liquidity signal.

### 2.2 Material financial risk disclosed on the company's own storefront

SpeedyBee is currently collecting a **54% prepaid tax on orders shipped to US addresses**, refundable against an official duty receipt if customs assesses less. This notice appears on multiple product pages — e.g. [Mario MiniX](https://www.speedybee.com/speedybee-mario-minix-modular-o4-lite-o4-pro-hd-digital-vtx-fpv-drone/) and [Bee25](https://www.speedybee.com/speedybee-bee25-2-5-inch-drone-hd-o3-air-unit-fpv/) — and is corroborated by third-party coverage. This is the single largest identifiable threat to SpeedyBee's direct-to-consumer economics: the US is a core FPV market, and a 54% prepaid surcharge on a price-led brand is close to an existential pressure on the direct channel. It also creates a structural incentive to push volume through US-domiciled distributors instead.

A second logistics constraint: batteries ship separately and only to nine countries (US, Germany, France, Switzerland, UK, Spain, Sweden, Italy, Australia), and standard postal air mail is unavailable on drone SKUs — express delivery is mandatory ([Bee25 product page](https://www.speedybee.com/speedybee-bee25-2-5-inch-drone-hd-o3-air-unit-fpv/)).

### 4.2 What this governance structure tells you

- **Yang Zongyuan is effectively the company.** In a PRC LLC, the 法定代表人 (legal representative) holds binding signature authority; combining that with 执行董事 (sole executive director, i.e. a one-person board in lieu of a full board) *and* 经理 (general manager) concentrates all governance, board and operational authority in one individual. Functionally he is founder, chairman, CEO and controlling shareholder simultaneously. At 92% ownership he also cannot be outvoted on any matter.
- **No board of directors exists.** The 执行董事 structure is the PRC alternative to a board, permitted for smaller LLCs. This confirms there is no investor board seat, no independent director, and no outside governance — consistent with the zero-funding finding.
- **Yan Wenjun's 8% is most plausibly a co-founder or key-employee stake**, though the registry does not state a role or title. There is no public information tying him to any operational function.
- **Yuan Xinxing (Finance Officer)** is the only named finance contact; no CFO title is used.
- **No published bios exist for any of the three.** I found no interviews, conference appearances, LinkedIn profiles, academic backgrounds, or prior-employment records for Yang Zongyuan, Yan Wenjun or Yuan Xinxing in any English- or Chinese-language source consulted. This is normal for Chinese component-industry founders who market a brand rather than a personality — a deliberate contrast with DJI's Frank Wang (汪滔) or Fat Shark's Greg French, both of whom have substantial public profiles.
- **No named CTO, COO, CMO or VP-level executives are public.** The engineering organization is anonymous.
- **Community-facing presence is institutional, not personal.** The brand runs a [SpeedyBee FPV YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@speedybeefpv1088) and communicates product changes through official statements (e.g. the image-only F405 V4 discontinuation notice reported by [Oscar Liang](https://oscarliang.com/speedybee-f405-v5/)), never through a named individual.

## 6. PATENTS AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 6.1 Patents — activity summary (individual documents not publicly retrievable)

**Count:** The Chinese business-information aggregator 爱查ICP records **6 patents (专利)** under 珠海市快蜂科技有限公司 in its intellectual-property section ([source](https://www.aichaicp.com/cd-91c66a46b7a78c6a34bf7fb14f7c1901.html)). The 水滴信用 record for the same entity does not display a patent section at all.

**Individual patent numbers, titles and filing dates could not be retrieved.** Google Patents returns JavaScript-rendered results that do not resolve to text via fetch; its XHR query endpoint returned HTTP 503; Espacenet and PatentGuru both blocked automated retrieval (403 / 468). Retrieving the six documents requires a direct session on **CNIPA's 专利检索及分析系统** (pss-system.cnipa.gov.cn) or a paid database (企查查 / 天眼查 / 智慧芽 PatSnap / Lens.org) searching applicant `珠海市快蜂科技有限公司`. I am flagging this as an unresolved gap rather than fabricating numbers.

**What can be said about the patent posture:**

- **Six patents is a low count** for a 132-person hardware company with 60+ SKUs. For calibration, DJI holds patents in the thousands. This is characteristic of the FPV component sector generally, where product cycles are 6–18 months, designs are frequently open-hardware-adjacent, and enforcement against Shenzhen/Zhuhai clone manufacturers is impractical — so firms compete on cadence, price and ecosystem rather than IP exclusivity.
- **Chinese filings of this profile are typically 实用新型 (utility models) and 外观设计 (design patents) rather than 发明专利 (invention patents).** Utility models in the PRC are granted without substantive examination in roughly 6–12 months, are cheap, and are the standard defensive instrument for mechanical hardware. SpeedyBee's likely filings are mechanical and layout claims, not algorithmic ones.
- **Probable subject matter**, inferred from the product features SpeedyBee markets as proprietary:
  1. **Wireless-configuration flight-controller architecture** — an integrated Bluetooth/WiFi 2-in-1 chip on the FC enabling app-based configuration *and* firmware flashing without a PC. This is the company's signature claim and the most likely invention-patent candidate. ([App page](https://www.speedybee.com/speedy-bee-app/), [F7 V3](https://www.speedybee.com/speedybee-f7-v3-flight-controller/))
  2. **Anti-shock fixed stack mounting structure** — the Master 5 series' stack mounting designed to isolate motor-induced vibration from the FC gyro.
  3. **Modular frame architecture** — the Master3X / Mario MiniX modular systems with independently replaceable plates, arms, PowerKits, VTX kits and Type-C extension modules. Design-patent territory.
  4. **Aluminium-alloy head / crash-protection assemblies** for O3/O4 air units on Bee25 and Master 3X.
  5. **Dual air-intake cooling** on the Bee25.
  6. **Meteor LED status-signalling system** — LED-encoded error, RTH, low-voltage and lost-drone states.
  7. **Multi-function adapter** combining FC configuration, ESC flashing, blackbox retrieval, voltage checking and 30W USB fast charging in one 35g device (Adapter 3).

- **Licensing/litigation:** no patent litigation, licensing agreement, infringement action or opposition proceeding involving SpeedyBee surfaced in any source consulted.

## 7. LATEST NEWS AND DEVELOPMENTS (2024–2026)

### 7.1 The F405 V4 discontinuation and the OX32 closed-source controversy (August 2025 → ongoing)

The biggest brand-level story of the period. On **30 August 2025**, SpeedyBee launched the **F405 V5 OX32** stack and issued an official statement (delivered as an image, not text) confirming the **discontinuation of the F405 V4** — until then arguably the single most widely deployed budget stack in 5" FPV. Price moved to **$74.99 from the V4's $65.99**. Source: [Oscar Liang](https://oscarliang.com/speedybee-f405-v5/).

The controversy is the ESC firmware. The V5's 32-bit ESC runs **OX32, a closed-source firmware**. Liang, the sector's most-read reviewer, wrote that he was "not thrilled about this move," noting OX32 is "very new" and "still largely untested by the community," and drew an explicit parallel to the earlier **BLHeli_32 loss of open-source status**. He argued SpeedyBee could have stayed on 8-bit hardware with open-source Bluejay, since 32-bit ESCs deliver little practical benefit to most FPV pilots, and flagged as "unclear" whether OX32 hardware can be reflashed to **AM32**, the open-source alternative.

Secondary complaints from the same coverage and its comment thread:
- **Mandatory activation**: first connection via the SpeedyBee App requires activating the FC and supplying an **email address** — unprecedented for an FPV flight controller. (Not required if the user only ever uses Betaflight Configurator.) This is the closest thing to a data-collection story in SpeedyBee's history.
- **Motor outputs cut from 8 to 4**, breaking hexa/octocopter builds. One commenter (Patrick) called the stack "unusable for my octocopter" and criticized the absence of an upgrade path.
- **INAV support reported missing in practice** despite being advertised (commenter Chris Nugent).
- **Conformal coating present but not waterproof**; hand-coating of solder joints still advised.
- Competitive defection recommendations surfaced in comments (HGLRC F460, JHEMCU H7 at similar prices).

This matters strategically: SpeedyBee's entire brand equity was built on *openness plus convenience*. The V5 keeps the convenience and trades away the openness, in a community that is unusually ideological about open-source firmware. Watch whether AM32 flashing becomes possible — that single technical outcome will likely determine whether this becomes a durable reputational problem or a passing complaint.

### 7.2 The 54% US prepaid tariff surcharge (2025–2026, ongoing)

SpeedyBee is collecting a **54% prepaid tax** on all orders shipped to US addresses, in response to new US tariff policy, refundable on presentation of an official duty receipt showing a lower actual assessment. This notice is live across product pages including the [Mario MiniX](https://www.speedybee.com/speedybee-mario-minix-modular-o4-lite-o4-pro-hd-digital-vtx-fpv-drone/) and [Bee25](https://www.speedybee.com/speedybee-bee25-2-5-inch-drone-hd-o3-air-unit-fpv/), and is corroborated by retailer commentary ([SoarSky](https://soarskyrc.com/blogs/technology-sharing/fpv-flight-controller-complete-guide-how-it-works-key-specs-amp-speedybee-full-lineup-comparison)). For a price-led brand this is the most consequential commercial development of the period and materially disadvantages SpeedyBee's direct channel versus US-stocked distributors.

### 7.3 Move to DJI O4 and modular architecture (2025–2026)

The current product generation is organized around **DJI O4 air units** (O4 Lite, O4 WA, O4 Pro) and around modularity as a design philosophy. The four "new"-tagged products on the homepage as of this research are all from this wave ([speedybee.com](https://www.speedybee.com/)):
- **Mario MiniX Modular O4 Lite/O4 Pro** ($2.90–$249.00 configurable)
- **Bee25 2.5" HD O3 Air Unit** ($11.99–$399.99)
- **Master 3X Modular O3/O4 Pro** ($139.99–$429.99)
- **Bee35 3.5" HD O3 Air Unit** ($169.99–$429.99)

The modular approach — individually purchasable plates, arms, PowerKits, VTX kits, motor guards, shells, and colorways down to $0.99 spare beepers — is a recurring-revenue play: sell the platform once, sell crash replacements forever. It also neatly dodges the tariff problem on complete-aircraft imports by moving volume into low-value spare parts.

### 7.4 Domestic silicon migration

The Mario MiniX ships an **F435-OX32-25A-AIO** board, and Chinese community build guides explicitly highlight "国产AT32飞控" (domestic AT32 flight controller) SpeedyBee builds ([ChinaFlier forum](https://uav.chinaflier.com/thread-34960-1-1.html)). The shift from STMicroelectronics STM32 to Artery AT32 parts is a supply-chain and cost story with geopolitical undertones, and it is happening quietly across the Chinese FPV sector.

### 7.5 Fixed-wing expansion matured

Having entered fixed-wing in December 2023 with the [F405 WING APP](https://oscarliang.com/speedybee-f405-wing-app/) ("currently the cheapest wing FC you can get"), SpeedyBee now fields two wing FCs (WING APP and WING MINI) running INAV/ArduPilot, and the WING MINI is distributed in China as a development board ([Spotpear](https://spotpear.cn/shop/SpeedyBee-F405-WING-MINI-Flight-Controller-Driver-Board-FC-ArduPilot-INAV-DJI-RC-FPV-Fixed-Wing-Airplane-Drone-Quadcopter.html)). This diversifies the company beyond multirotor and into the ArduPilot community — a different, more technical, less price-sensitive audience.

### 7.6 Hiring into AI

The **AI Application Engineer** posting (2026-08-13, RMB 10–15k/month, [爱查ICP](https://www.aichaicp.com/cd-91c66a46b7a78c6a34bf7fb14f7c1901.html)) among 81 open roles is the clearest forward-looking signal in the dataset. For a company whose differentiator has always been software-mediated setup, the plausible read is AI-assisted tuning, automated blackbox diagnosis, or conversational configuration in the app — extending the original 2018 "leave the laptop at home" thesis.

### 7.7 Component-level trend context

The **ICM-42688-P** gyro (32kHz sampling, lower noise floor than MPU6000) has become the 2025–2026 standard, and SpeedyBee has adopted it across F405 AIO V2, F405 V4, F405 V5, WING APP and WING MINI ([SoarSky](https://soarskyrc.com/blogs/technology-sharing/fpv-flight-controller-complete-guide-how-it-works-key-specs-amp-speedybee-full-lineup-comparison), [UAVMODEL 2026 guide](https://blog.uavmodel.com/top-5-fpv-flight-controllers-in-2026-betaflight-vs-inav-vs-ardupilot/)). SpeedyBee remains a fast follower on silicon rather than a leader.

### 7.8 Quality perception — the persistent counter-narrative

Not all coverage is favorable, and it would be misleading to omit this. Reviewer comment threads contain repeated durability complaints: one commenter on the Master 5 V2 review alleged over **$1,500 in losses** from SpeedyBee electronics failures and advised buying other brands ([Oscar Liang](https://oscarliang.com/speedybee-master-5-v2-drone/)); another on the V5 piece cited receiver compatibility problems and frame quality ([Oscar Liang](https://oscarliang.com/speedybee-f405-v5/)). These are third-party comments, not reviewer assessments — the reviewers themselves have been broadly positive on value. The pattern is nonetheless a real brand risk for a company competing on price. Counterbalancing it: SpeedyBee demonstrably responds to feedback, e.g. adding a BZ-181 M10 GPS option to the Master 5 V2 after launch in response to a reader request (ibid.).
