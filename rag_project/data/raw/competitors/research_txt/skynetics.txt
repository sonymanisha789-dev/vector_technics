## 0. DISAMBIGUATION — READ FIRST

## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

Legal name: SKYNETICS PRIVATE LIMITED. CIN U26109KA2023PTC174313. Incorporated 30 May 2023. Registered address: Sahakaranagar, Bangalore North, Karnataka 560092. HQ Bengaluru.

Founded 2023 by engineers with satellite/space-electronics backgrounds from TU Delft, ETH Zurich, KIT, IISER, and ISRO. Founding story: "born out of the dreams of graduates from the Netherlands and Germany." Strategic thesis: Indian space-sector import-substitution — "The parts that stayed closed for decades are the parts we build."

Mission: "to develop highly miniaturized and cost-effective embedded solutions... pushing technology beyond limits for extreme scenarios." Homepage tagline: "Space-Grade Technology. Made in India." — "50+ subsystems flown, ISRO-empanelled."

Size: reported figures range 7 (Tracxn) to 9-12 (LinkedIn/Exa) employees, LinkedIn self-declared band 11-50. Realistic estimate: 7-12 people, grew from ~2 to ~9 over the prior year (+350% YoY). LinkedIn followers grew from 3,518 (Jun 2026) to 5,000 (Sep 2026).

Footprint: 3 countries, 3 ground stations (Netherlands, Dubai, Bangalore), India as primary hub. 3 ISRO nanosatellites carrying its hardware, 2 complete satellites delivered.

Certifications/posture: AS9100D in progress (NOT yet certified); SCOMET export-control compliant; DGFT IEC issued; IN-SPACe authorization issued; ISRO-empanelled (claimed). Notable gap: selling flight hardware before AS9100D certification completes.

Flight heritage claimed: INS-1A, INS-1B, INS-1C (ISRO nanosatellites — subsystems), Gaganyaan 2024 (bio-sensor module), CubeSat-1U and CubeSat-3U delivered end-to-end. Aggregate claims: 50+ flight-qualified subsystems at TRL 9, 100% mission success across 50+ deployments, MTBF 12,500 hrs, 4 patents. CAUTION: INS-1A/1B/1C launched 2017-2018, years before the 2023 company existed — this is founder-team heritage carried from prior ISRO/TU Delft work, not company track record.

Named clients/partners: ISRO, DRDO, ADA, URSC, ISTRAC, IN-SPACe (govt/agency); L&T, Bellatrix Aerospace, Kepler Aerospace, Shyena Tech Yantra, Aidin Technologies (industry); IIST, Dayananda Sagar University, Edutech4Space (academic). Also sells to universities for space-environment simulation platforms.

## 5. PRODUCTS

33 products across 6 lines. Full datasheets gated behind "Request Capability Statement" contact forms; patented-tech briefs available only under NDA.

**SKYCOM Series — Long-Range Communication (6 products).** Flagship, DRDO Best Innovation Award winner, "indigenous design replacing imported alternatives." Includes SKYCOM-1550 (sub-GHz, 100km, 5W), SKYCOM-4025 (100km, 5W industrial), SKYCOM-7092 (ISM band, 5W), SDR Communication Platform (1MHz-18GHz, >150Mbps full duplex — most technically ambitious item), X-Band Resilient Communication Module (coming soon), Resilient Communication Module (100MHz-6GHz frequency-hopping, drones) — the latter two tied to the pending 4th patent, the clearest anti-jam/contested-spectrum defence differentiator.

**Satellite Systems (8 products).** Full vertically-integrated 1U CubeSat bus (1U-24U range claimed): 1U CubeSat platform (<1.33kg), SKYSATCOM-1500 (VHF/UHF comms, flight proven), CubeSat Power System (MPPT), CubeSat Battery System (Li-Ion/BMS), CubeSat OnBoard Computer (ARM Cortex-A53, rad-tolerant — a relatively high-performance choice implying edge-AI ambitions), CubeSat Solar Panel (GaAs, 30% efficiency, deployable), CubeSat GNSS Module, 1U CubeSat Structure. Very few 7-person companies can claim a full in-house bus stack.

**Ground Stations (4 products).** VHF/UHF Ground Station ("highest gain yagi antennas installed in India," ISRO-supported), S-Band Ground Station (2Mbps, 2.4m dish), S/X-Band Dual Ground Station (100Mbps, 3.7m dish), Custom Ground Station. Sites: Netherlands, Dubai, Bangalore. End-to-end mission capability (bus + comms + ground) is a strategically stronger position than pure component vending.

**Custom Electronics / Embedded (5 products, NRE line).** RF Range Extender (drone-based walkie-talkie relay), Underwater Robotics, Underwater Communication System (acoustic/optical), HAPs Communication System (high-altitude platform), RF GaN Power Amplifier (500W, DC-10GHz — a genuinely hard, high-value defence/EW component). Company deliberately spans seabed to orbit on one RF/signal-processing core competence.

**IoT & Sensors (8 products, largest line).** NTN-01 (satellite IoT sensor node), SDC-01 (satellite-drone C2, BVLOS — the strongest bridge to the UAS market), TAG-WL (satellite wildlife/bird tracking), LORA-100 (>100km LoRa), GGY-BIO (ISRO Gaganyaan bio-monitoring — marquee credential, human spaceflight hardware), EDG-ML (ultra-low-power edge ML), LORA-AI (AI-powered LoRa sensor), FPGA-AI (FPGA+AI chip board).

**UAV & Drone Technologies (2 products — smallest line).** Sky Sentinel and Sky Scout — both explicitly "reference stacks"/demonstrators for multi-rotor platforms (RF + edge AI), not saleable aircraft. Confirms Skynetics is a Tier-2 subsystem supplier to drone OEMs, not a drone OEM itself.

Announced/roadmap: SKYSATCOM-7985 (X-band satcom module, launched Sept 2026 at Bengaluru Space Expo, not yet in web catalogue); ELINT/COMINT payloads (shown to ISRO Chairman as "upcoming launches" — major strategic move into signals intelligence); next-gen GNSS modules; next-gen receiver designed to a sensitivity target set by AVM Manu Midha, DG Defence Space Agency.

## 6. PATENTS

Four patents total — three granted (Netherlands only), one pending.

1. **NL2028379B1** — "Method and apparatus for determining angle of attack and wind speed" — Granted, NL. Traces to Narayana's 2021 "Hermes" IEEE RA-L paper. Wind-energy-harvesting self-powered sensor; relevant to UAV flight control / HAPs.
2. **NL2024953B1** — "Systems and methods for infrared sensing" — Granted, NL. Traces to "LOCI"/PIR papers (2015, 2020). Privacy-preserving passive-IR multi-person localization; applications in building automation, security, perimeter sensing.
3. **NL2031744B1** — "Channel sensing circuit and apparatus comprising the circuit" — Granted, NL. Traces to LoRa/RF-harvesting MAC papers (2022, 2025). Enabling tech behind LORA-100 and high-density LoRaWAN scaling.
4. **"Beyond Frequency Hopping — A True Resilient RF Communication"** — Filing in progress, no number/jurisdiction yet. IP behind the Resilient Communication Module and X-Band Resilient Communication Module. Strategically the most significant patent — aimed at defence procurement, aligned with current RF hiring push and the Defence Space Agency's receiver-sensitivity challenge.

Portfolio assessment: small (4), young, geographically narrow (Netherlands-only — no US/EP/PCT/Indian grants found, a material weakness given the company's Indian defence/ISRO market and "Made in India" positioning). Research-derived, not product-derived — all granted patents cover TU Delft-era (2020-2022) sensing/channel-access research, not the satellite-bus subsystems that make up most of current sales. Assignment to Skynetics Pvt Ltd (vs. TU Delft or the inventor personally) is unverified since the patents predate the May 2023 incorporation.

## 8. SYNTHESIS

Skynetics is a 3-year-old, 7-12-person, unfunded Bengaluru deep-tech hardware company commercializing a TU Delft space-IoT research programme, with genuine (largely founder-carried) ISRO/Gaganyaan flight heritage, a full in-house 1U CubeSat bus, an award-winning long-range RF transceiver line, and three Netherlands patents. Currently pivoting toward defence RF (resilient/anti-jam comms, ELINT/COMINT) with active senior engagement from ISRO, IN-SPACe, and the Defence Space Agency.

Strengths: rare vertical integration for its size, real flight heritage, DRDO award, near-complete regulatory posture, tight research-to-product mapping, well-timed against India's space-sector liberalization, unusual 3-country footprint.

Weaknesses: unfunded with tiny revenue while announcing an RF/ELINT roadmap that needs capital; extreme key-person risk (one founder's IP); patent estate is Netherlands-only and possibly not corporately assigned; AS9100D not yet certified while selling flight hardware; zero third-party press validation; flight-heritage claims predate the company by 5-6 years; 33 products across 6 lines at ~7-12 headcount implies most SKUs are reference designs/low-volume NRE rather than stocked inventory.
