# RESEARCH REPORT: "ZERODRAG" (drone/UAS)

## 0. DISAMBIGUATION — read first

Three distinct things carry the "Zerodrag" / "zero drag" name. Only one is a drone/UAS company:

1. **ZeroDrag Technologies Private Limited (India)** — the subject of this report. An Indian deep-tech UAV avionics and drone-electronics manufacturer, founded 2022, registered in Nagpur, Maharashtra, operating from IIT Delhi's Research & Innovation Park and a Noida office. Web: [zerodrag.in](https://zerodrag.in/). This is the only entity plausibly a competitor to Vector Technics.
2. **ZeroDrag™ (inline skate bearings, US)** — [zerodrag.com](https://zerodrag.com/) is a completely unrelated sports-bearing brand that says it has "been in the sports bearing business for over 20 years," selling bearings for fitness skating, roller hockey and marathon racing. No aerospace connection whatsoever. Note the trap: the .com is NOT the drone company; the drone company is the .in.
3. **"zero drag" as an aerodynamics marketing phrase** — appears in UAV copy from unrelated firms (e.g. PteroDynamics' Transwing VTOL marketing around low-drag configurations, [pterodynamics.com](https://pterodynamics.com/)). Not a company name.

No US or European defence contractor named "Zerodrag" was found in any source. Everything below concerns entity #1.

Note also the company's **former name/brand: "DroneAdda."** CB Insights still files the company under that name ([cbinsights.com/company/droneadda](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/droneadda)), and TheCompanyCheck lists two associated brands for the same CIN: "DroneAdda" and "Zerodrag." If you are deduplicating a database, DroneAdda and Zerodrag are the same legal entity.

**Mission and positioning.** ZeroDrag does not build drones. It builds the avionics and core electronics layer that drones run on — flight controllers/autopilots, ESCs (motor controllers), GNSS/navigation, telemetry and radio links, and payload-integration hardware — and sells them B2B to drone OEMs on an explicitly OEM-agnostic, cross-platform basis. The founder's formulation, quoted repeatedly in coverage: **"We're not building drones. We're building what every drone needs."** ([startupsamadhan.com](https://startupsamadhan.com/zerodrag-raises-6-5-crore-seed-transition-vc-drone-avionics-india/))

The strategic pitch is import substitution and supply-chain sovereignty: India has scaled domestic airframe manufacturing under PLI and related schemes, but the avionics inside those airframes remain overwhelmingly Chinese-sourced (DJI, Holybro, SpeedyBee and similar). ZeroDrag positions itself as the indigenous alternative for that layer, serving defence, agriculture, logistics, industrial inspection, and the FPV/racing community. Coverage consistently notes rising inbound interest from **US and European** manufacturers and governments seeking non-Chinese component sources ([yourstory.com](https://yourstory.com/2026/07/zerodrag-indian-drone-hardware), [dronefederation.in](https://www.dronefederation.in/media-hub/zero-drag-raise-funds-to-build-drone-avionics-layer)).

**Go-to-market.** Direct D2C via a Shopify storefront at zerodrag.in, plus six named **Authorised Channel Partners** in India: ZBOTIC, Vega Drones, TujoRC, Robu.in, drkstore.in, and Quadkart. Also resold by UAVGarage ([uavgarage.com/product-brand/zerodrag](https://uavgarage.com/product-brand/zerodrag/)). B2B/OEM sales are direct, with **custom design and white-labelling offered for bulk orders**. Warranty is 6–12 months depending on product line (12 months on the core boards).

## 6. PATENTS AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**Bottom line: no granted patents or published patent applications attributable to ZeroDrag Technologies Pvt. Ltd., Shantanu Bhede, or Amit Nimje could be located in any accessible public source.**

Methodology and what was checked:
- Google Patents keyword and XHR query endpoints for "zerodrag" — returned no retrievable results (the endpoint served 503/anti-automation responses on repeated attempts, so this is an inconclusive rather than confirmed-zero check).
- General web searches explicitly combining "zerodrag" with patent / trademark / design registration returned **zero organic results** — the query surfaced only an unrelated LegalZoom advertisement, with the engine reporting "No more results found."
- **Tracxn's company profile lists no patent or IP section** for ZeroDrag, and no IP is referenced in any of the ~15 news articles and database profiles reviewed.
- MCA filings show no IP-related charges or licensing disclosures.

What can be said about the IP posture:
- The company describes its flight controller as **"proprietary"** (Amit Nimje, F6S profile) and markets a distinctive **"fully solder-less" modular mechanical/electrical interface** across the Nova, Warp and Strix boards. That solder-less interconnect is the most obviously patentable/design-registrable element of the portfolio, and an Indian design registration for it would be plausible — but nothing is findable in accessible sources.
- The firmware layer is explicitly **open-source-derived** (Betaflight, INAV, ArduPilot, AM32), so the defensible IP is necessarily hardware/design and manufacturing know-how, not software.
- **DPIIT "Recognised Startup"** status is confirmed, which in India confers patent-fee rebates and expedited examination — a mild indicator that filings may exist in the unpublished 18-month window, particularly around the April 2026 LOC8 GNSS launch and the H7 controller.
- **Trademark:** the brand is used with a claim mark in commerce ("Zerodrag," "Nova," "Warp," "Strix," "LOC8," "Nexus," "Aurora," "Catalyst"). Indian trademark registry records were not retrievable through accessible sources; TrademarkElite's India search blocked automated access. Note the collision risk with the unrelated US **ZeroDrag™** skate-bearing brand, which is in a completely different Nice class, so likely non-conflicting.

**Caveat for the record:** absence of evidence here is weak evidence of absence. Indian Patent Office records (ipindiaservices.gov.in public search) and WIPO Patentscope are not accessible to automated retrieval and were not checked directly; a manual IPO search against applicant name "ZERODRAG TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED" and inventor names Bhede/Nimje would be the correct follow-up. For a four-year-old, ₹7.7 Cr-funded hardware startup, having zero granted patents would nonetheless be entirely unremarkable.

## 9. SUMMARY ASSESSMENT / DATA-QUALITY NOTES

- ZeroDrag is a **very early-stage, sub-₹1 Cr-revenue, ~15–17 person Indian hardware startup** with ~₹7.7 Cr (~$0.83–1.23M) raised to date and no disclosed valuation. It is not a peer to a scaled avionics OEM on financial metrics; it is a peer on *product category* — flight controllers, ESCs, GNSS, radio links.
- Its differentiation is **geopolitical and supply-chain, not technological**: Made-in-India, non-Chinese, cross-platform, open-firmware-compatible, white-labellable. The technology stack is largely open-source firmware on in-house-designed hardware.
- **Trajectory is clearly upward-market**: FPV hobby components (2024) → prosumer H7 flight controllers (2025) → enterprise RTK GNSS and heavy-lift propulsion (2026) → stated intent toward heavy-lift and eVTOL avionics (2026–2028).
- **Red flags for diligence:** FY25 revenue declined 34% to ₹27.6 lakh; ROE −84.78 and net margin −134 indicate meaningful losses against a tiny top line; the entire storefront was showing "Sold out" at the time of research; no executives beyond the two founders are publicly named; no verifiable patent estate; and the flagship defence claim (320+ km/h interceptor drone) appears only on the company's own LinkedIn.
- **Source conflicts to be aware of if this feeds a RAG index:** HQ is variously Nagpur / New Delhi / Noida (all real, different functions); total funding is $828.92K (Inc42) vs $830K (CB Insights) vs $1.23M (Tracxn); round count is 2 vs 7; the ₹6.5 Cr seed converts to both "$680K" and "$780K" across outlets; authorised capital reads ₹66 lakh (current) vs ₹15 lakh (stale Tofler); and the company appears under the legacy name **DroneAdda** in CB Insights.
